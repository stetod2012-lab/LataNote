FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, including the study tutor, handwriting to text, live collaboration and device sync, is free. There are no paid plans.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start writing. Your notebooks, recordings and planner are saved in your browser on this device. Use Settings → Download a backup any time.

How do I get my notes on another device?

Open LataNote on both devices, go to Settings → Sync with another device, and type the 6-digit code from one device into the other. Notes merge in both directions, and newer edits win.

How does live collaboration work?

Open a note, choose Collaborate live from the ••• menu, and share the link. Everyone with the link edits the note together in real time, including handwriting. Keep your tab open during the session.

Where does the study tutor send my notes?

Nowhere. If your browser has a built-in on-device AI model, as recent versions of Chrome can, the tutor uses it. Otherwise it uses LataNote’s own engine. Either way, your notes stay on your device.

How do I go full screen?

Press F or the full-screen button at the top of a note. For an app-like experience, install LataNote from Settings → Install LataNote, or in Safari use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Can I make the text bigger, or change how it looks?

Yes. Open Appearance (the palette button in the sidebar) to choose from 12 themes, including animated ones like Aurora, Ocean waves and Sakura, plus a High contrast theme. You can also make all text larger, switch to Atkinson Hyperlegible (a font designed for readers with low vision), or reduce motion.

Does it work with a stylus?

Yes. LataNote reads pen pressure from styluses such as Apple Pencil, Surface Pen and S Pen. Turn on Stylus only so a resting palm doesn’t leave marks.
