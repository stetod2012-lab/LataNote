Powerful tools that don’t get in your way

Every tool is one tap away, with no complicated workflows to learn. Use one, or use all of them on the same page.

Natural handwriting

Pressure-sensitive pen, highlighter and eraser with smooth, low-latency ink. Turn on palm rejection so only your stylus draws.

Typing & documents

Headings, lists, checklists, tables, links and images. Structure a full document, or type a quick line between your sketches.

Drawing & diagrams

Lines, arrows, rectangles and ellipses on blank, lined, grid, dotted or Cornell paper. Sketch it as fast as you think it.

Audio that syncs to ink

Record an entire lesson while you write. On playback, your ink appears in step with the audio, so you can hear what was said as each note was written.

PDF & image annotation

Academic planner

Classes, tasks, exams and events on one calendar, each linked to the notes that go with it. Upcoming deadlines always in view.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start writing. Your notebooks, recordings and planner are saved in your browser’s local storage on this device. You can export a full backup any time and restore it on another device.

Does it work with a stylus?

Yes. LataNote reads pen pressure from styluses such as Apple Pencil, Surface Pen and S Pen. Turn on Stylus only in the drawing toolbar so a resting palm doesn’t leave marks.

Where does the study assistant send my notes?

Nowhere. The built-in assistant runs entirely in your browser, analyzing your text to produce summaries, flashcards, quizzes and answers. Your notes never leave your device.

Can I annotate PDFs?

Yes. Import a PDF and every page is added to your note as a background you can write, draw, highlight and type over. You can also import images such as photos of a whiteboard.

How do I share or print my notes?

Use the note menu to export a self-contained HTML file, print or save as PDF, or share a .latanote file that someone else can import into their own LataNote.
